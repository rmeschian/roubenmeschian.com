About

I help businesses turn data into actionable insights by designing end-to-end systems — from databases and data infrastructure to intuitive web applications — and leveraging the latest advances in AI to unlock even greater value.

I was one of the founding engineers of Anzo at Cambridge Semantics, where I spent sixteen years building the client-side platform that every Anzo application ran on. Today I'm frontend architect and core full-stack engineer on Siemens Graph Studio, an enterprise knowledge-graph platform with billions of triples in production.

I'm an early and heavy adopter of AI-assisted engineering — spec-driven development with Claude Code, Copilot-driven modernization, and hands-on work on AI agents and custom Claude skills. Clear thinking turns into precise specifications, and precise specifications turn into high-leverage prompts.
